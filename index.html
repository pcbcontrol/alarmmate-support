Compatible sensors

Two sensor modules for learning and experimenting with AlarmMate Sensors.

Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense

Compact module with Bluetooth, motion and sound sensing.

Seeed Studio

ESADE Sensor V3+

Compact module for practical work in ESADE’s «SGI: Mobile app programming» course, combining motion and sound sensing with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connectivity.

Both modules require the corresponding AlarmMate firmware. For the XIAO nRF52840 Sense, you can load the firmware from the app over USB. Available features depend on the module and its configuration.
