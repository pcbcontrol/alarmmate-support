1. Contact support

For help with the AlarmMate app for iOS, or to make a privacy request, email mutton-purge1s@icloud.com.

Developer and responsible person: Francesc Roig.

2. What to include

  • Your app version/build and iOS version.
  • The sensor model and the connection method you were using: Bluetooth, local Wi-Fi, or Tailscale.
  • The steps you followed, what happened, and what you expected.
  • If helpful, a screenshot you have reviewed before sharing.

Do not send passwords, private keys, access tokens, or unnecessary personal data.

3. Before reporting a connection issue

  1. Check that the sensor has power and that Bluetooth is enabled.
  2. Check AlarmMate’s Bluetooth, local-network, and notification permissions in iOS Settings, as relevant.
  3. Check the sensor’s actual connection state in AlarmMate. Stored settings alone do not confirm that a disconnected module received a command.
  4. For network features, check that the module and the selected service are available.

Background delivery depends on iOS, connectivity, permissions, and the selected transport. Avoid closing the app from the app switcher when you need Bluetooth monitoring.

4. Manage your information

You can clear event history in History or export it as a CSV file. Removing a sensor, deleting its history, and deleting external accounts are separate actions. Some saved credentials may persist. See the privacy policy for details, retention, and privacy-request options.

5. Academic use and limitations

AlarmMate Sensors is intended for academic use, learning and sensor experiments. Detection and alerts may fail or be delayed. Read the use, safety and limitations notice before relying on a configuration.
