1. Academic use and experimentation

AlarmMate Sensors is intended for academic use, learning and experimentation with sensors. It provides supplementary information for exploring sensor behaviour, connections and alerts. Evaluate whether it is suitable for your particular activity.

Do not rely on AlarmMate as the sole safeguard for people, health, animals, property or other situations where a missed or incorrect alert could cause serious harm. It does not replace appropriate safety equipment, professional monitoring or emergency services, and does not guarantee prevention of theft, damage or loss.

2. Detection and alerts can fail

Readings may be inaccurate, events may be missed, notifications may arrive late or not arrive, and false alarms can occur. A displayed or saved setting does not always confirm that a disconnected module received the command.

Operation depends on compatible hardware and firmware, sensor placement and calibration, power and battery condition, permissions, iOS restrictions, Bluetooth range and interference, network access and external services. These conditions can interrupt or degrade operation. Continuous, error-free operation and delivery of every event are not guaranteed.

3. No additional warranties

To the extent permitted by law, AlarmMate is supplied as is and as available, without additional express or implied warranties. Mandatory warranties, remedies and consumer rights remain unaffected.

4. Use the system responsibly

Use the app and accessories lawfully and only on equipment or in places where you have the necessary authority and permissions. Respect other people's privacy. Follow the equipment instructions, verify the setup and test the relevant functions under the conditions in which you plan to use them.

Check power, connection status and actual alert delivery regularly. Arrange independent safeguards and a response plan when consequences matter. Stop using a configuration that behaves unexpectedly or could create a hazard, and seek appropriate assistance.
